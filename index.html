WHAT IS IRIUM?

Irium is an independent SHA-256d mine-only proof-of-work blockchain engineered for maximum decentralization and survivability. It is not a fork of any existing chain and was built from scratch in Rust with its own networking, consensus, and peer logic.

Launched on January 5, 2026, Irium follows transparent issuance with no ICO, no presale, and no premine. The protocol is designed to operate without DNS infrastructure and to maintain consensus even in hostile or infrastructure-restricted environments.
