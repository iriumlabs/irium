Settlement-First Direction

SETTLEMENT

Irium (IRM) is a mineable, settlement-first blockchain for escrow, agreements, and proof-based commerce.

The live chain is already an independent SHA-256d Proof-of-Work network built from scratch in Rust. The broader direction is trust-minimized commercial settlement with objective, rules-based outcomes.

What It Means

Settlement-first means outcomes, not only transfers

Payment chains move units from one address to another. A settlement-first chain is built around whether a defined commercial outcome can be expressed, anchored, proved, and resolved under objective rules.

Escrow

Funds should be able to move under defined release or refund conditions instead of trust-heavy manual coordination.

Agreements

Agreement hashes and related references can anchor real business terms to immutable chain state.

Proof-based commerce

The long-term aim is commerce that settles from signatures, timestamps, verifiable proofs, and explicit timeout rules.

Why Payments Are Not Enough

Transfers alone do not solve commercial coordination

Many real transactions need more than a payment rail. They need deposits, milestones, refunds, delivery proofs, or a way to define what happens if one side does not respond.

Contractor workA transfer does not describe milestones, holdbacks, or proof of completion.
Deposits and preordersMerchants and buyers often need refund conditions, not just a one-way payment.
OTC tradingPeer-to-peer deals are safer when escrow and objective release logic are available.
Business settlement flowsCommercial agreements often require staged releases, defaults, and evidence paths.
Proof handlingConsensus must stay objective, so only verifiable proof paths belong in settlement logic.
No subjective judges in consensusHuman arbitration should remain outside consensus rather than becoming a protocol backdoor.

Roadmap Phases

Progression from base layer to commercial settlement

Phase 1 • Live

Core independent PoW foundation

Independent chain, mineable SHA-256d Proof-of-Work, Rust implementation, DNS-free bootstrap direction, peer resilience, public explorer, public pool, calculator, fair launch properties, and locked founder vesting.

Phase 2 • In development

Settlement foundation

Agreement-hash anchoring, native escrow and settlement outputs, timeout refunds, and simple flows for OTC escrow, deposits, and contractor settlement.

Phase 3 • Planned

Objective proof automation

Objective proof validation, approved attestors, signature-verifiable proof paths, no-response auto-settlement, and milestone proofs remain planned work.

Phase 4 • Planned

Commercial infrastructure

Templates, merchant and contractor flows, recurring settlement direction, portable reputation tooling, APIs, and broader business integrations.
